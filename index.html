about | عني

My name is Rashid Dar. I'm an independent operator providing a variety of services at the intersection of policy, philanthropy, and culture, especially as pertains to the broader Muslim world. Throughout my career, I have spent time in a number of sectors, from think-tanks and large grant-making foundations to diplomacy and academia. I am currently based out of the beautiful city of Istanbul.

I recently finished up a role as a Program Officer for the John Templeton Foundation (JTF), where I handled a multi-million dollar grant-making portfolio known as Islam, Science, and Society While at JTF, I traveled the world, structuring and managing dozens of large-scale research projects aimed at seeding astute and forward-thinking Islamic thought.

Prior to this role, I worked as a researcher focusing on political Islam at the Brookings Institution in Washington, DC, specifically within the Project on U.S. Relations with the Islamic World. Before that, I was part of a highly-discreet Track II initiative trying to bring calm to the Syrian Civil War. I attended Columbia University (SIPA, MIA 2015) the University of Wisconsin - Madison (L&S, BA 2011).

Presently, I am seeking consulting and advisory roles where I can leverage my unique skillset and background to add value for business, philanthropy, and policy—especially those actors looking to expand their work in the Muslim world.

If you'd like to speak further, please contact me to collaborate. Alternatively, you can schedule a meeting with me.
